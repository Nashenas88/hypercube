# shaders/*.wgsl

WGSL shaders sharing `Transform4D` (`rotation_matrix`, `viewer_distance`, `sticker_scale`, `face_gap`, `face_gap_4d`, `elapsed_seconds`), `CameraUniform`, and `StickerInstance` structs plus 4D math functions, all defined once in `math4d.wgsl` and pulled into each pipeline shader via `naga_oil`'s `#import` (composed in `renderer.rs` through a `naga_oil::compose::Composer`, since WGSL itself has no import mechanism). `sticker_common.wgsl` similarly shares `LightUniform` and `HighlightingUniform` (and their bound globals) across every sticker material shader. `classic_shader.wgsl` is the Classic theme's sticker material (flat per-`kind` colors from a `KindColors` uniform); `elemental_shader.wgsl` is the Elemental theme's, switching on `kind` to pick an animated material per element (helpers `hash11`/`hash21`/`value_noise1`/`fresnel` shared via `elemental_common.wgsl`) and driven by `Transform4D.elapsed_seconds`.
